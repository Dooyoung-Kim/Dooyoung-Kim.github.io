Human-Centered AI · Human Factors

HEART Model

Human Experience-Aware Response and Trajectory

While world models learn how the world evolves, HEART explores how human experience unfolds within it.

HEART is a human-centered AI and human factors research agenda for multimodal modeling of how people perceive, attend, anticipate, act, and respond within situated environments. Rather than predicting a single optimal action, it represents multiple plausible experience and response trajectories shaped by context, memory, intention, and interaction.

XRMemory captures human experience; HEART investigates how that experience unfolds. Building on XRMemory, HEART moves from capturing and replaying experience toward representing its internal structure, temporal evolution, and plausible future trajectories.

Experience-Aware Representation

Represent human experience as an evolving state of perception, attention, expectation, intention, action, feedback, and memory.

Plausible Human Trajectories

Model multiple ways a person may experience and respond to the same situation, rather than reducing human behavior to one optimal next action.

Situated Human–AI Interaction

Help AI systems account for a person’s context, perspective, prior experience, and interaction history when responding or acting.

Current Direction

Our initial studies use frozen pretrained video representations to examine whether meaningful interaction states and transitions emerge in egocentric and XR experience data. This diagnostic stage precedes the development of a dedicated multimodal HEART architecture.
