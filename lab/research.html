XR for Physical AI

Giving Physical AI Spatial Memory

I study how Physical AI can connect what it observes now with past experiences tied to the same places, objects, and equipment. This research explores how XR can preserve those experiences and human know-how in their spatial and task context. The goal is to recall what matters now and adapt it into actions suited to the robot and its surroundings.

Store Experiences in Context

Preserve what happened, where, and under which task conditions. Link experiences and human know-how to places, objects, and equipment, with access to the original spatiotemporal records.

Recall from the Current Scene

Use the current scene, object states, and task goals as cues to recall relevant experiences from that place or equipment, together with the human know-how needed to interpret them.

Adapt Experience into Action

Use recalled experiences to guide decisions and actions. Adapt them to the robot’s body, the present spatial layout, and feedback as the task unfolds.
