Privacy.

This site uses optional analytics to understand which pages and apps are useful. Analytics stays off until you choose to allow it.

What is collected

  • Page views, approximate session engagement, device category, and general traffic source.
  • Internal and external link interactions.
  • For Life Quest: app opens, active time, selected controls, and high-level actions such as creating or completing a quest.

What is never sent to analytics

Names, email addresses, Firebase user IDs, goal statements, quest titles, character names, free-text form entries, and precise location are not included in analytics events.

Storage and provider

Analytics events are processed by Google Analytics 4. Retention follows the settings of the site's Analytics property. Raw event export may be enabled later in Google BigQuery for controlled, longer-term analysis.

Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored only in this browser. Use the “Analytics settings” button on any page to change it. Declining analytics does not affect the site or Life Quest.
